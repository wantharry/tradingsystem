# Strategy 3: Volume Breakout Momentum

**Family:** Breakout | **Best Regime:** Uptrend, High Volatility  
**Difficulty:** Intermediate  
**Expected Win Rate:** ~40-50% | **Target R:R:** 2:1

---

## What Is This Strategy? (Plain English)

Imagine a crowd pushing against a door. Most of the time, the door holds. But when enough people push at once (high volume), the door breaks open and the crowd rushes through. **Breakout trading** catches that moment.

When a stock bursts through a price level it couldn't break for weeks, *with unusually high volume*, it signals genuine new demand (or supply) entering the market — not just noise.

---

## Entry Rules

1. **Price breaks the 20-period high (long) or low (short):**
   - Close > highest close of past 20 days = long breakout
   - Close < lowest close of past 20 days = short breakout

2. **Volume confirms conviction:**
   - Today's volume ≥ 1.5× the 20-day average volume
   - Without high volume, breakouts fail more than 60% of the time

3. **Candle size confirms momentum:**
   - Today's trading range (High - Low) ≥ 1× ATR
   - Small candles on breakout = weak, likely to reverse

4. **Enter:** Next day market open

---

## Exit Rules

**Stop:** Below the breakout level (where it broke from), 1.5× ATR below entry  
**Target:** 2× the distance from entry to stop  
**Time Stop:** 15 days — breakouts either work fast or fail

---

## Why This Strategy Has Edge

Breakouts work because they represent **supply/demand imbalances**. When a stock breaks to new highs with triple the usual volume, large institutions are buying — and they keep buying for days. We're essentially riding their coattails.

Volume is the key filter. Academic studies show volume-confirmed breakouts succeed ~48% of the time vs ~28% for low-volume breakouts.
